## Notes to the editor

What I would add if I had it:

- **A real example.** One question from a BIRD or Spider training database with the gold SQL, a correct sample that differs from it in form, a wrong result and an execution error. It would define the label and the pair faster than the prose does. I did not invent one.
- **Counts by question.** The packet gives pairs, not questions. "Fewer questions give a pair" follows from the pair counts only if every such question gives exactly $M$ pairs. The number of questions with a pair per round, split into all-correct and all-wrong for the rest, would be direct evidence. I gave no cause for the drop, as the ceiling asks.
- **The time limit.** The value is not in the packet; it belongs here or in Section 4.
- **How the $M$ pairs are drawn.** The packet does not say whether the two draws are independent, so the text does not say whether a question can give the same pair twice.

What I doubt:

- **"The adapter and the count that weights it."** The packet says the client sends the adapter "and nothing else", but the server cannot compute $w_k$ without $|\mathcal{P}_k^{t}|$. I wrote that the count leaves too, as $n_k$ does in a FedAvg SFT round. Please check against the code; if the weights are computed another way, the last sentence of the Aggregation paragraph changes.
- **Two sentences reasoned from the formula, not from a result.** Local training starts at $m_\theta = 0$ because the start and the reference are both $\theta^{t-1}$. The first term can fall without the likelihood of $y^{+}$ rising. If the ablation in Section 6 shows the NLL term matters little, the second should be softened.
- **"So the samples follow the distribution of the model itself"** is my gloss on temperature 1 with no cut.
- **"Which trains with the same two terms on one machine"** compresses the packet's description of CodeLutra ("a DPO term plus a supervised term"). Check that "the same" holds for the supervised term.
- **Units of `\setting{adapter.mb}`.** I wrote `~MB` after it; remove that if the macro prints the unit.
- **"top-k" is set in roman** so that it is not read as the client index $k$.
- **Length.** The text is roughly 800 words against the 600 asked. The two extra no-pair cases (empty tables, gold SQL that does not run) and the cut-off sample could move to Appendix B, which would recover about 70; the rest would have to come from the loss paragraphs.
- **No sentence on differential privacy.** The data-isolation statement is made by saying what stays and what leaves; a disclaimer reads better in the setting or the limitations.
