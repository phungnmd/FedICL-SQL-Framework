- **Abstract (`00_abstract.tex`)** — *Context, limitation, proposed framework and mechanism, principal quantitative results, implication* (~250 words; no floats, no citations): Federated training of a shared small language model (`\setting{model}`, `\setting{model.size}` parameters) for Text-to-SQL under client data isolation; limitation of FedAvg SFT (single gold SQL target, unqueried client databases, no supervision on incorrect model outputs) and FedDPO (static external pairs, fixed reference model across rounds); `\method`, a federated learning framework for Text-to-SQL that follows `\setting{rounds.warmup}` warm-up rounds with `\setting{rounds.pref}` preference rounds of client-side query execution against the gold SQL result, per-round on-policy pair construction, and DPO-plus-NLL optimization against each round's global model; `\gain{bird}{fedavg}{4}{k2}{4}` EX over FedAvg SFT at `\setting{rounds.total}` rounds on BIRD dev (`\ex{bird}{k2}{4}`\% vs. `\ex{bird}{fedavg}{4}`\%; `\gain{bird}{fedavg}{3}{k2}{4}` over peak FedAvg SFT) and `\gain{spider}{fedavg}{4}{k2}{4}` EX on Spider test (`\ex{spider}{k2}{4}`\% vs. `\ex{spider}{fedavg}{4}`\%), while halving execution errors (`\nerr{bird}{fedavg}{4}` to `\nerr{bird}{k2}{4}`; `\nerr{spider}{fedavg}{4}` to `\nerr{spider}{k2}{4}`); client databases provide supervision at unchanged per-round communication (`\setting{adapter.mb}` adapter plus integer weight).
- **Paragraph 1** — *Context and significance* (~110 words; no floats): Natural-language access to relational databases via Text-to-SQL (`yu2018spider`, `li2023bird`); external schema and query exposure in prompting pipelines over proprietary LLMs (`pourreza2023dinsql`, `gao2024dailsql`); local fine-tuning and on-premise deployment enabled by open-weight small language models (SLMs, 0.5B to `\setting{model.size}` parameters; `li2024codes`, `sheng2025slmsql`).
- **Paragraph 2** — *Specific setting* (~120 words; no floats): Scarcity of databases and annotated question--SQL pairs within single organizations paired with data isolation across organizations; collaborative training of one shared, locally deployed SLM via federated learning (`mcmahan2017fedavg`); scope definition stated once (*federated Text-to-SQL* as federated training of the Text-to-SQL model; `zhang2023fl4sp`) and prevailing paradigm of FedAvg SFT over a LoRA adapter (`hu2022lora`, `ye2024openfedllm`).
- **Paragraph 3** — *Limitations of existing approaches* (~180 words; no floats): Two limitations sharing one structural cause: (a) FedAvg SFT (`mcmahan2017fedavg`, `ye2024openfedllm`, `zhang2023fl4sp`) supervises only one gold SQL query per question (`sec:method`), entering client databases solely as prompt schema text without executing gold or model-generated queries and providing no signal against incorrect model outputs; (b) FedDPO (`rafailov2023dpo`, `ye2024openfedllm`) trains over static external preference pairs against a single fixed reference model across all rounds without an NLL anchor, failing to target the evolving errors of the trained model; shared cause in the requirement of database execution for SQL verification, where the database resides exclusively at the client.
- **Paragraph 4** — *Key insight* (~75 words; places and cites Fig. 1, `fig:idea`): Single-sentence core observation that executing model-sampled queries on the client database and comparing their execution results with that of the gold SQL query converts one gold annotation into multi-sample execution-derived supervision over the model's own correct and incorrect queries (`sec:supervision`), contrasted visually with gold-only supervision inside the client boundary (`fig:idea`, without repeating the figure's concrete query example).
- **Paragraph 5** — *Proposed framework* (~175 words; no floats): Introduction of `\method`, a federated learning framework for Text-to-SQL (`sec:method`, `sec:overview`), with four components mapped to the two limitations: *supervised warm-up* (`\setting{rounds.warmup}` FedAvg SFT rounds establishing correct-sample coverage), *client-side execution labeling* (answering limitation (a) by sampling from the current global model and labeling via local execution against the gold result), *on-policy pair construction* (answering limitation (b) by renewing preference pairs and setting the reference model to the round's global model at every preference round, aligning one preference-optimization iteration with one communication round), and a *preference objective* combining the DPO preference term (`rafailov2023dpo`) with a token-averaged NLL term on the correct sample, while keeping per-round upload identical to FedAvg SFT (`\setting{adapter.mb}` LoRA adapter plus one integer aggregation weight).
- **Paragraph 6** — *Empirical validation* (~140 words; no floats): Headline evaluation on `\setting{model}` (`\setting{model.size}` parameters; `hui2024qwen25coder`) across five non-IID clients (`sec:setup`) at `\setting{rounds.total}` equal communication rounds (`\setting{rounds.warmup}` warm-up, `\setting{rounds.pref}` preference): statistically significant EX gains over FedAvg SFT of `\gain{bird}{fedavg}{4}{k2}{4}` on BIRD dev (`\ex{bird}{k2}{4}`\% vs. `\ex{bird}{fedavg}{4}`\%, $p = {}$\pval{bird}{fedavg}{4}{k2}{4}) and `\gain{spider}{fedavg}{4}{k2}{4}` on Spider test (`\ex{spider}{k2}{4}`\% vs. `\ex{spider}{fedavg}{4}`\%, $p = {}$\pval{spider}{fedavg}{4}{k2}{4}; `tab:main`, `sec:main`), FedAvg SFT plateau by round 3 (`\gain{bird}{fedavg}{3}{k2}{4}` over peak FedAvg SFT on BIRD dev), and statistically significant ~50\% reductions in evaluation execution errors on both BIRD dev (`\nerr{bird}{fedavg}{4}` to `\nerr{bird}{k2}{4}`) and Spider test (`\nerr{spider}{fedavg}{4}` to `\nerr{spider}{k2}{4}`; `sec:analysis`).
- **Paragraph 7** — *Contributions* (~150 words excluding list markup; 4-item `itemize` list, no floats): Four parallel items pairing contribution and significance without repeating Paragraph 6 digits: (1) client databases as client-exclusive execution-derived supervision unexploited by federated fine-tuning, yielding to our knowledge the first federated training method for Text-to-SQL that uses it (`sec:supervision`); (2) the `\method` framework, aligning one on-policy preference-optimization iteration and global reference-model update with each communication round at unchanged per-round client upload of the adapter and its integer weight (`sec:method`); (3) empirical validation of the EX gain over FedAvg SFT on BIRD dev and Spider test (`sec:main`), its origin in halved execution errors and valid predictions over non-gold tables or columns (`sec:analysis`), component ablations (`sec:ablation`), and client compute cost (`sec:cost`); and (4) public release of code, federated client splits, and evaluation predictions for every table row.
- **Caption of Fig. 1 (`fig:idea`, `figures/fig_idea.pdf`)** — *Visual contrast of client-side supervision paradigms* (~95 words; excluded from section word count): Comparison on one training question within the privacy boundary of client $k$: `\panel{a}` FedAvg SFT minimizes NLL on the single gold SQL query from question and schema text while leaving the client database unqueried; `\panel{b}` `\method` executes `$N = {}$\setting{pref.samples}` model-sampled queries on the client database against the gold SQL result to separate correct samples from incorrect results and execution errors, forming preference pairs optimized with DPO plus NLL; in both paradigms, raw data and execution artifacts remain local and only the LoRA adapter (with its aggregation count) is transmitted to the server for FedAvg aggregation (all abbreviations expanded at first figure occurrence).
