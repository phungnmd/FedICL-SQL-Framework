Organizations that hold relational databases can fine-tune a shared small language model (SLM) for Text-to-SQL by federated learning with low-rank adaptation (LoRA) while their databases and annotated questions remain on their premises. Federated averaging with supervised fine-tuning (FedAvg SFT) trains each client on the gold SQL query of each question with a negative log-likelihood (NLL) loss and never executes a query of the model on the client databases, and federated direct preference optimization (FedDPO) trains each client on its fixed preference dataset against one fixed reference model. We propose \method, a federated learning framework for Text-to-SQL that trains on the execution of the model's own queries at each client. After \setting{rounds.warmup} warm-up rounds of FedAvg SFT, each client in a preference round samples SQL queries from the current global model, labels them by executing them on its own databases against the result of the gold SQL query, and trains on pairs of a correct and an incorrect sample with a direct preference optimization (DPO) term, whose reference model is the global model of the round, plus an NLL term. With \setting{model} (\setting{model.size} parameters), \fact{split.bird.a0.1.k5.clients} clients and \setting{rounds.total} rounds, \method{} reaches \ex{bird}{k2}{4}\% execution accuracy (EX) on BIRD dev and \ex{spider}{k2}{4}\% on Spider test, \gap{bird}{fedavg}{4}{k2}{4} and \gap{spider}{fedavg}{4}{k2}{4} points above FedAvg SFT at the same number of rounds. On BIRD dev, it is \gap{bird}{feddpo}{4}{k2}{4} EX points and \tsgap{bird}{feddpo}{4}{k2}{4} test-suite accuracy points above FedDPO run on the first execution-labeled pairs of \method, and \gap{bird}{k2}{4}{rft}{4} EX points above a variant trained without the preference term at the same settings. Against FedAvg SFT, execution errors fall from \nerr{bird}{fedavg}{4} to \nerr{bird}{k2}{4} on BIRD dev and from \nerr{spider}{fedavg}{4} to \nerr{spider}{k2}{4} on Spider test, while incorrect results do not fall. A client sends the same adapter as in FedAvg SFT in every round, so communication is unchanged, and the preference rounds add client computation for sampling, labeling and training.
