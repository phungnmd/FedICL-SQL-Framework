\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Fine-tuned models for Text-to-SQL}\label{sec:rel-slm}

Open models for Text-to-SQL are fine-tuned on the training sets of the
benchmarks, often after further pre-training or with synthetic data.
CodeS~\cite{li2024codes} continues the pre-training of StarCoder models
on a SQL-centric corpus before fine-tuning on
each benchmark, and OmniSQL~\cite{li2025omnisql} fine-tunes
Qwen2.5-Coder-Instruct models of 7B to 32B parameters on about 2.5
million synthetic samples together with the training sets of Spider and
BIRD. SLM-SQL~\cite{sheng2025slmsql} studies the model sizes of this
paper, 0.5B to 1.5B parameters. It fine-tunes on 916K synthetic samples,
trains further by reinforcement learning on an execution reward and
votes over 64 samples, and reports
67.08\% EX on BIRD dev with its 1.5B model. Our models train on the
benchmark training sets alone and decode greedily, and their absolute
levels are well below this figure. All of these models are trained on
data held in one place.

\subsection{Preference optimization and execution feedback}\label{sec:rel-pref}

Direct preference optimization (DPO)~\cite{rafailov2023dpo} trains a
model on pairs of a preferred and a dispreferred response without a
reward model, through the negative log-sigmoid
of the scaled difference of their log-ratios under the trained model and
a reference model, usually the SFT model.

Execution first served Text-to-SQL at inference, where
LEVER~\cite{ni2023lever} reranks sampled programs with a verifier that
reads their execution results. In training, Group Relative Policy
Optimization (GRPO) is applied with rewards computed by
execution~\cite{papicchio2025think2sql,yao2026arctic,kulkarni2025reinforcing}.
Liu et al.~\cite{liu2025cotdpo} sample 16 queries per BIRD training
question from the SFT model, pair a correct with an incorrect sample and
train with DPO alone against the SFT model as reference. With SQL-only
outputs, this lowers BIRD dev EX under greedy decoding and majority
voting for 6 of their 10 models. ExCoT~\cite{zhai2025excot} continues
DPO in on-policy rounds on new samples of the model, labeled again by
execution, and rejection sampling fine-tuning~\cite{yuan2023rft} trains
on the correct outputs of the model without a preference term.

For a likelihood term on the preferred response beside the DPO term,
published results range from a large gain to none. Iterative reasoning
preference optimization~\cite{pang2024irpo} pairs correct with incorrect
chains of thought sampled from the current model and trains with the DPO
term plus a length-normalized NLL term, reaching 73.1 on GSM8K after one
round against 61.8 with DPO alone. Liu et al.\ report a limited gain from
an added SFT term on SQL-only outputs, and Lanchantin et
al.~\cite{lanchantin2025bridging} no benefit from an added NLL term.

The closest work is CodeLutra~\cite{tao2024codelutra}. At one site, it
samples ten queries per question from the current model, labels each by
comparing its execution result with that of the gold query, and trains
for four iterations with the DPO term, whose reference model is the
model of the previous iteration, plus a likelihood term on the correct
sample, so that the pairs and the reference model are renewed at every
iteration. It reports 76.6\% EX on Spider with this loss against 17.2\%
with the DPO term alone. In \method, the labels are
computed at each client on databases that the server never sees, one
federated round is one iteration for all clients with the global model as
their common reference model, and the server averages the adapters by
the pair counts. The preference rounds start from a model trained in
FedAvg SFT warm-up rounds, and each question contributes
\setting{pref.pairs} pairs with an NLL term averaged over tokens. Table~\ref{tab:related} sets the
closest methods beside \method.

\subsection{Federated fine-tuning of language models}\label{sec:rel-fed}

FederatedScope-LLM~\cite{kuang2024fsllm} and
OpenFedLLM~\cite{ye2024openfedllm} fine-tune LLMs with LoRA under FedAvg
and other algorithms. In
federated SFT, a client trains on the responses of its dataset, not on
outputs of the model. OpenFedLLM also introduces FedDPO, in which every
client trains with the DPO loss alone on its own preference dataset
against a reference model fixed throughout the federated process.
FedPDPO~\cite{zhu2026fedpdpo} and pFedDPO~\cite{jiang2026pfeddpo}
personalize federated DPO for clients whose preferences differ, and both
train on fixed preference datasets. In FedBiscuit~\cite{wu2025fedbiscuit},
clients train binary selectors on their preference data, and the server
labels with them pairs that its initial model generates once. In
federated reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards, a client
samples from the current model and trains with GRPO on a reward for
correct answers, to math and medical questions in
PubSwap~\cite{nayak2026pubswap} and to math and code tasks in
Fed-GRPO~\cite{guo2026fedgrpo}. Neither has been applied to Text-to-SQL,
and this paper does not evaluate such a policy-gradient method with an
execution reward.
Averaging the two LoRA factors separately does not average their
product~\cite{sun2024ffalora}, and \method{} and its baselines keep the
separate average.

For federated semantic parsing, Zhang et al.~\cite{zhang2023fl4sp}
fine-tune T5-base with FedAvg, FedOPT and FedProx on the gold SQL
queries of eight single-domain clients and score predictions by exact
match without executing a query, reaching 62.55 macro-averaged exact
match against 70.32 for centralized training. KGRFL~\cite{xu2024kgrfl}
fuses client models that translate questions into SPARQL. To our knowledge, no earlier
method for federated training of Text-to-SQL models uses the databases
of the clients for execution labels.
