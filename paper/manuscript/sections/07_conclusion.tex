\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

In \method, after supervised warm-up rounds, each client labels samples
of the global model by execution on its databases and trains on pairs of
correct and incorrect samples with a DPO term and an NLL term. At equal
rounds and communication, it is \gap{bird}{fedavg}{4}{k2}{4} EX points
above FedAvg SFT on BIRD dev and \gap{spider}{fedavg}{4}{k2}{4} on Spider
test, with about half as many execution errors while incorrect results
do not fall, and on BIRD dev it is above FedDPO trained on its own first
pairs. These results come from one training run per model of at most
\setting{model.size} parameters, with different training settings in the
two kinds of round. Partial participation, labels checked on several
contents of a database, and what an adapter reveals remain to be
studied.
