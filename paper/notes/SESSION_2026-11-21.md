# Session of 2026-11-21: review of the method and its way to a Q2 paper

Written 2026-11-21 18:05 (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh). A record of one working
session: what the owner asked, what was found, what was decided, what is
proposed and what is still open. Every count below was read from the
committed predictions; the tables with all tests are in `LAB_LOG.md`
(entries of 2026-11-21). The estimates of section 3 are judgement, not
measurement.

## 1. What was asked

1. Read the recent results as a professor of data science would and give
   a direction for the method.

3. What do the directions under development add to that?
4. Let two agents in the role of professors discuss and research better

## 2. What was found

**BIRD dev by official difficulty, 2 rounds** (simple 925, moderate 464,
challenging 145):

| Row | EX | Simple | Moderate | Challenging |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FedAvg SFT | 42.70 | 50.49 | 31.25 | 29.66 |
| DPO + NLL from the base student (k = 0) | 43.02 | 50.70 | 35.13 | 19.31 |
| 1 SFT round, then DPO + NLL (k = 1) | 44.26 | 50.38 | 37.07 | 28.28 |

k = 0 loses the challenging level (3 against 18 questions, p 0.0015);
k = 1 gains on the moderate one (51 against 24, p 0.0024). The
preference signal exists only on questions with both correct and wrong
samples, and the base student has no correct sample for 42.8% of the BIRD
training questions (Spider: 12%).

**Spider, the method against FedAvg SFT round 4, on execution accuracy
(EX) and on test-suite accuracy (TS).** p is the exact McNemar test
against FedAvg SFT. The suite covers the 20 Spider dev databases: dev,
Syn, Realistic and 408 of the 535 DK rows; Spider test and BIRD have
none.

| Set | EX: FedAvg SFT | EX: k = 0 | EX: k = 2 | TS: FedAvg SFT | TS: k = 0 | TS: k = 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spider test (2,147) | 74.71 | 78.02 (3.1e-5) | 78.20 (6.4e-7) | | | |
| Spider dev (1,034) | 75.15 | 77.27 (0.082) | 77.66 (0.021) | 67.99 | 67.21 (0.58) | 70.41 (0.027) |
| Syn (1,034) | 59.09 | 62.09 (0.024) | 62.96 (0.0006) | 51.84 | 51.35 (0.76) | 55.51 (0.0004) |
| Realistic (508) | 67.72 | 71.26 (0.063) | 70.47 (0.10) | 58.86 | 57.09 (0.40) | 62.20 (0.036) |
| DK (EX on 535, TS on 408) | 59.63 | 67.10 (2.4e-5) | 63.55 (0.017) | 53.19 | 55.39 (0.31) | 55.15 (0.28) |

On EX both lines are above FedAvg SFT on all five sets. On the suite the
line from the base student has no gain, and the line with the SFT stage
keeps 2.42, 3.67 and 3.34 points on dev, Syn and Realistic; there k = 2
is also above k = 0 (p 0.010, 0.0014, 0.0067). "The SFT stage gives no
gain on Spider" holds for test EX alone. The suite numbers were computed
on the Mac with the project's judge and are not a committed result yet.

**What a round breaks.** BIRD dev, both rounds from FedAvg SFT round 1:
the DPO + NLL round fixes 173 questions and breaks 90 (79 into wrong
results); the SFT round fixes 98 and breaks 39. Spider test, one round
from FedAvg SFT round 2 on the same samples and pairs:

| Round | Correct answers written differently | Broken | Fixed |
|---|---|---|---|
| NLL on the model's own correct samples | 6.0% | 21 | 59 |
| Preference term alone | 36.5% | 168 | 157 |
| DPO + NLL | 19.4% | 74 | 146 |
| FedAvg SFT round 3 | 14.4% | 48 | 61 |

The preference term makes the fixes and the breaks; the NLL term holds
the rewriting down.

## 3. Chance of a Q2 paper (estimates)

CMC and JIIS are Q2 on Scimago and Q3 on JCR by the sources read for
`JOURNAL_BAR.md` (JIIS is Q2 on one of them); the quartiles were not
checked on Scimago or Clarivate themselves. Which list the owner's
requirement counts is the largest open factor.

| | Submitted as it stands | After the missing parts | If BIRD separates and a second model keeps the gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| CMC | about 35% | 55 to 65% | about 75% |
| JIIS | about 15% | 30 to 40% | about 50% |

Submitted to both in turn on the Scimago list: about 70%, about 85% in
the last column. If JCR Q2 is required: about 30%.

Three things hold the number, none of them a weak method: the gain on a
second and third model is not measured; BIRD is not separated from
FedAvg SFT at 2 rounds (k = 1: +1.56, p 0.13; about +2.1 is needed); and
a complete paper is still accepted in about 75% of cases at CMC and 50%
at JIIS. CMC accepts 25% of its submissions.

Missing against what the two journals publish (`JOURNAL_BAR.md`): two
more models, a cost table, one federated factor varied (alpha or number
of clients), FedProx and local-only baselines.

## 5. The discussion of the two reviewer agents

Reviewer A read as a federated-learning researcher, reviewer B as a
Text-to-SQL researcher. Three rounds: independent positions with a
literature search, critique of each other, a signed joint text. Both
only read the repositories.

- A brought the fix and break counts, and the point that the comparison
  has to hold at equal compute (BIRD: k = 1 costs 10 h 40, four FedAvg SFT
  rounds 11 h 20). A's own first idea (clients without gold SQL) was
  dropped by the owner; A's second (two updates per round, summed) gave
  way to one local loss.
- B brought the test-suite reading and the table by loss term. B's stricter
  positive became a control and B's hint a table row.
- The table of section 4 is what both signed.
- Not agreed: an evaluation-only probe that adds the updates of
  `bird_sft/round_2` and `bird_k1/round_1` to their parent (go at 45.3
  or 46.0), and the weight of the gold rows (1 or 4).
- Their own estimates of a separated gain on BIRD: 0.20 to 0.35 for the
  gold target in every round, 0.55 for `bird_k2/round_1`.

The session's view differs in one place: the probe that adds the two
updates is skipped. Both reviewers train the one-loss form first whatever
it shows, and it needs new code.

One row is missing for a method with an SFT stage: federated DPO as
published has no BIRD line from an SFT start. On-policy rounds differ
from fixed pairs only from the second DPO round, so it needs
`bird_k2/round_2` and its fixed-pair twin.
