## P2.4b — equal-private-pass schedule screen (conditional)

The first screen uses three total private data passes in every final endpoint:

- `A>K>A[e2]`: two terminal local epochs, one terminal aggregation;
- `A>K>A>A`: two one-epoch terminal rounds, aggregate after each;
- `A>K>A>K>A`: two KD/private cycles;
- `A>A>A`: no-KD pure-FL control.

The external three-terminal-epoch schedule is **not** this screen. Open E3 only
if E2 remains unsaturated without erasing BIRD.

Keep every method-stage `A` at one local epoch. `A[e2]` is a diagnostic that
delays aggregation across two passes; it is not the default method. This makes
the screen distinguish three effects at fixed private-data exposure: local
depth (`A[e2]`), aggregation frequency (`A>A`), and a second teacher transfer
(`A>K>A`). Starting with E3 would confound all three and add an unmatched
private-data pass.
