# FedLS-SQL — protocol-v2 target outline

Working title and final method subtitle remain open until P2.3. The advisor's
umbrella title/question remains a valid directional target.

Target contribution package:

- a dataset-correct federated LLM-to-SLM NL-to-SQL framework with explicit
  public/private/evaluation roles;
- a reproducible EX gain over lightweight pure FL;
- matched evidence identifying whether the gain comes from teacher-generated
  targets, soft KD, federated optimization, or their interaction;
- bidirectional Spider/BIRD evidence clarifying transfer scope;
- adapter communication, SLM deployment, and precisely bounded structural
  privacy evidence.

The exact loss, aggregation algorithm, and teacher-use policy are placeholders
until protocol-v2 experiments select them.
