# FedLS-SQL — active KD decision

## Current baseline

The protocol-v2 comparison uses two distinct teacher signals:

1. **SeqKD:** train with cross-entropy on execution-verified SQL generated by
   the teacher.
2. **Hinton soft KD:** add
   `T^2 KL(p_teacher^T || p_student^T)` on the same target-token positions.

The default soft-KD control is `T=2`, `lambda_ft=1`, and `lambda_kd=1`.
Teacher logits are full-vocabulary, detached, and may be cached because the
selected target sequence and prompt are fixed. The experiment fingerprint
records the temperature and cache metadata records
`kd_objective=hinton_forward_kl`.

## Decision

- Execution-verified SeqKD remains the hard-target baseline.
- Hinton forward KL replaces reverse KL as the only active soft-logit baseline.
- Reverse KL, skew-RKL, and KID are removed from the active implementation and
  experiment queue. Their previous analysis is retained only in
  `paper/archive/method_reviews_2026-09/KD_METHOD_REVIEW_RKL.md`.
- No additional KD objective is opened until both protocol-v2 transfer
  directions complete the matched T1 ladder.

## Required causal ladder

```text
Pure FL
  vs matched public-gold CE
  vs execution-verified teacher-target CE (SeqKD)
  vs the same SeqKD target CE + Hinton forward KL
```

EX is the promotion metric. Hinton FKL becomes part of the final method only if
it improves paired EX over SeqKD under the same initialization, selected pool,
training budget, and evaluation protocol. Otherwise the paper method remains
execution-verified SeqKD after federated aggregation.

## Deferred research

After the corrected two-direction baseline closes, a new KD method may be
proposed only in response to an observed failure mode. Candidate families
include adaptive divergence, on-policy distillation, structured SQL signals,
and privileged-evidence transfer. Each requires one matched control, a fixed
budget, an EX promotion threshold, and a stop rule.
