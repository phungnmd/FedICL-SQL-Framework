# FedLS-SQL — KD method review

> Status (2026-09-20): research review; P2.5 closed KID and
> `PIPELINE_NEXT.md` owns the active matched SeqKD/selected-gold gate. Nothing below becomes part of the method until it is
> implemented, compared with its matched control, and recorded in `LAB_LOG.md`.

## 1. Implemented KD baselines

Protocol v2 implements two teacher channels. The teacher is Qwen2.5-Coder-7B
(4-bit, zero-shot, frozen); the student is Qwen2.5-1.5B with LoRA.

1. **SeqKD:** CE on execution-verified teacher SQL. BIRD public keeps
   5,319/9,428 rows; Spider public keeps 7,251/8,659 rows.
2. **Hinton forward KL:** full public-gold CE plus
   `T^2 KL(p_teacher^T || p_student^T)` on gold-prefix target tokens, `T=2`,
   using a cached full-vocabulary teacher. Canonical runs use
   `lambda_ft = lambda_kd = 0.5`; the trainer default is 1/1.

Reverse KL, skew-RKL, and the old protocol-v1 KID are archived in
`../archive/method_reviews_2026-09/KD_METHOD_REVIEW_RKL.md`. The 5,319-row
SeqKD+Hinton hybrid was retired on 2026-09-12. Those old negative results are
not protocol-v2 evidence. The fresh protocol-v2 KID implementation used 20%
one-pass student rewriting followed by clean-gold CE and reverse KL against
the frozen teacher on rewritten prefixes. It is now a completed negative
ablation: terminal KID is statistically tied with terminal SeqKD on all five
sets despite about 21.1 GPU-hours for its public stage. GKD was closed before
publication because autoregressive online rollout was too costly.

## 2. Evidence

All values are seed-0 EX (%), evaluation batch size 16 unless stated.

### Hinton versus gold CE

Same FL parent, same 9,428 BIRD public rows, one public epoch.

| Hinton − gold CE (pp) | Spider | Realistic | SYN | DK | BIRD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| End of public stage (`A>K`) | +3.29 | −4.33 | +0.67 | +4.49 | +4.56 |
| After terminal private stage (`A>K>A`) | +0.09 | −0.19 | +0.87 | −0.56 | +1.50 |

After the terminal stage no difference is paired-significant
(`p = 1.000/1.000/.439/.749/.102`).

### SeqKD versus gold on the same selected rows

No terminal private stage.

| Direction | Gold CE | SeqKD | Delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| BIRD public → Spider private, Spider EX (batch 8) | 56.09 | 57.64 | +1.55 |
| Spider public → BIRD private, BIRD EX | 20.73 | 24.45 | +3.72 |

SeqKD is the only teacher channel with a positive sign in both directions. Its
terminal stage reaches 64.99/57.68/54.55/50.28/28.42 EX on
Spider/Realistic/SYN/DK/BIRD. The matched selected-gold terminal control is the
remaining causal gate before attributing this gain to teacher-generated SQL.

### SeqKD versus KID

At the terminal endpoint, KID minus SeqKD is
+0.48/−0.59/−0.39/0.00/+0.52 points on the same five sets. No difference is
paired-significant. KID therefore does not justify its online teacher cost and
does not open clean RKL or MiniLLM follow-ups.

### Reading

`A>K>A` beats `A>A` by 4.06 Spider and 14.54 BIRD points, but `A>K[ce]>A`
matches it. The current gain is explained by extra public SQL training plus
private consolidation, not by the teacher.

## 3. Why Hinton ≈ gold CE here

Hypotheses 1–2 have direct evidence; 3–5 are untested.

1. **The teacher adds little beyond gold on this pool.** Every public row has
   gold SQL, and Hinton scores only gold prefixes. The teacher is zero-shot:
   its greedy SQL matches gold execution on 5,319/9,428 rows (56.4%). On the
   remaining rows, KL may pull the student toward wrong table or column
   choices. Teacher and student share the Qwen2.5 family; Rethinking OPD
   (2026) reports same-family 1.5B and 7B teachers are nearly
   indistinguishable from the student's perspective.
2. **The terminal private stage erases the difference.** One Spider-private
   epoch removes the pre-stage gap on the four Spider sets; BIRD keeps about a
   third of it.
3. **The pre-stage advantage may be regularization.** Soft targets behave like
   adaptive label smoothing. There is no label-smoothing or self-anchor
   control. AdamW is nearly invariant to total loss scale, so 0.5 versus 1.0 CE
   weight is not a learning-rate change; the KL term is the real difference.
4. **The signal may be diluted.** KL is averaged over all target tokens, and
   most SQL tokens are near-deterministic keywords or punctuation. Not measured.
5. **The SeqKD gain may be target alignment, not teacher knowledge.** Selected
   teacher SQL is execution-equivalent to gold; 4,053/5,319 rows differ only in
   the SQL string. Correct SQL generated by the student itself could have the
   same effect (SDFT, ACL 2024).

## 4. Literature map, 2025–2026

| Family | Idea | Representative work | Fit for FedLS-SQL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offline logit KD (current) | KL on fixed prefixes | Sparse Logit Sampling (ACL'25), Bridge-Garden (2026) | Low: already ≈ gold CE; fixed offline data risks teacher hacking |
| On-policy KD (OPD) | student samples, teacher scores each token | GKD (ICLR'24), Thinking Machines OPD, Rethinking OPD (2026) | High: signal on the student's own errors |
| OPD with verifiable reward | gate KL by correctness | Reward-Gated OPD, OPDVR, SPOT (2026) | Very high: SQL has an executor and public gold |
| Improve teacher first | spend labels on the teacher, then transfer densely | Sparse-to-Dense (MSR 2026) | High: explains the weak zero-shot teacher |
| Privileged self-distillation | same model with the answer in context acts as teacher | SDFT (ICML'26), OPSD, SD-Zero (2026) | Medium: less forgetting, but in-context learning is weak at 1.5B |
| Token selection | train only high-entropy or high-divergence tokens | TIP, Revisiting OPD (2026) | High for cost: most SQL tokens are easy |
| Offline approximation of OPD | sample and score once, or reweight | Lightning OPD, Distribution-Corrected Offline Distillation (2026) | High if GPU time is limited |
| Small-model Text-to-SQL | teacher/synthetic SFT plus GRPO on execution reward | SLM-SQL, FINER-SQL, Arctic-Text2SQL-R1, EXPO-SQL | Reviewers will expect a teacher-free RL baseline |
| FL knowledge retention | clients distill from the global model | FedNTD (NeurIPS'22), FedGKD | Applicable to the terminal private stage |

### Findings that match the observed failures

1. **Sparse-to-Dense (MSR 2026).** Reported order: transfer from an untrained
   teacher < GRPO directly on the student < transfer from a teacher improved
   with the labels. Removing the improved teacher cost 7.8 MATH points,
   removing the forward-KL warmup 1.7, removing OPD 3.3 (abstract). FedLS-SQL
   sits in the lowest tier: zero-shot teacher, public labels fed straight to
   the student. The existing CE/Hinton public stage can serve as the warmup.
2. **RL's Razor (2025) and SDFT (ICML'26).** Forgetting tracks the KL shift
   from the starting model. SFT and off-policy updates move far; on-policy
   updates stay close. This matches the public stage costing Realistic −12 and
   SYN −5, and the terminal stage erasing teacher-specific gains while repairing
   that damage. Caveat: *Denser ≠ Better* (2026) finds dense self-distillation
   can forget more than GRPO, so drift must be measured, not assumed.
3. **Unmasking OPD and Reward-Gated OPD (2026).** Teacher guidance is most
   reliable on incorrect student outputs and noisy on correct ones; a teacher
   can also prefer a wrong answer. Hinton on gold prefixes sees only correct
   states, and nothing filters the 43.6% of rows the teacher does not solve.
4. **Teacher hacking (ICML'25).** Repeated passes over fixed offline teacher
   data teach shortcuts; online data mitigates it. More offline Hinton epochs
   carry this risk.

## 5. Candidate method: execution-gated on-policy distillation

```text
for each public prompt x (BIRD with evidence):
  y_1..y_n ~ student(x)                       # n = 2–4 short SQL samples
  r_i = EX(y_i, gold) in {0, 1}               # existing executor
  d_t = log p_T(y_t | ctx_T, y_<t) - log p_S(y_t | x, y_<t)
  a_t = max(d_t, 0) if r_i = 1 else min(d_t, 0)
  loss = -sum_t a_t * log p_S(y_t | x, y_<t) + lambda * CE(gold)
```

- **Correct SQL:** only raise tokens the teacher prefers. A correct query in a
  different style is not penalized.
- **Wrong SQL:** only lower tokens the teacher dislikes. This localizes errors
  (column, join) without clause-level execution.
- **Gold CE term:** keeps the hard-label anchor; Bridge-Garden reports that
  mixing hard and soft labels beats either alone.

### Teacher ablation

| Teacher | Teacher training | Question answered |
|---|---|---|
| T0: 7B zero-shot | none | plain OPD baseline |
| T1: 7B with gold SQL in its prompt | none | does a teacher that knows the answer remove the 43.6% problem? |
| T2: the 1.5B student with gold SQL in its prompt (OPSD) | none | is the LLM needed at all? |
| T3: 7B QLoRA-tuned on BIRD public | yes | does the sparse-to-dense principle hold here? |

Rethinking OPSD (2026) finds answer-in-context teachers hurt thinking models
but help instruction-tuned models; Qwen2.5-Instruct is the latter.

### Required control

**C-RL:** the same rollouts and reward with no teacher (RFT or GRPO). If T1 or
T3 does not beat C-RL, the LLM is not needed for dense signal. This control
also supplies the teacher-free RL baseline reviewers will ask for.

### Reusable code

- Student sampling: `fedicl_sql/models/student.py` (`generate_samples_scored`).
- Teacher scoring: `fedicl_sql/models/teacher_local.py` (`score_logits`).
- Read-only SQL execution and Spider/BIRD evaluators.
- 4-bit model loading for teacher and student.

Still to write: the rollout/reward loop, the gated loss, and an `opd` method in
the stage chain.

### Claim boundary

OPD with verifiable reward is an active 2026 trend, so the paper cannot claim
a new loss. A defensible claim combines the federated public/private boundary,
execution as the gate on teacher signal for SQL, an answer-aware teacher, and
measured evidence that on-policy public transfer damages the private domain
less.

## 6. Proposed screen (not queued)

- **S0a — cache audit, no training.** From the existing logit cache, measure
  teacher probability of the gold token, top-1 agreement, entropy, and where KL
  mass falls by token type (keyword, identifier, literal) and by
  teacher-correct versus teacher-wrong rows. Tests hypotheses 1 and 4.
- **S0b — weight interpolation, evaluation only.** Interpolate merged weights
  between `A>K` and `A>K>A` (alpha 0.3 and 0.5) for both CE and Hinton. If the
  Hinton gap reappears on the frontier, retention is the bottleneck.
- **S0c — OPD signal check, no training, about 500 public rows.** Sample four
  SQL queries per row from the full-gold CE T1 adapter
  (`artifacts/protocol_v2/p22d_spider_private_fullgold_hinton_t1/full_gold_ce_s0/round_1/m_g`),
  execute them, and score them with T0, T1, and T2. Measure how well teacher
  log-probability separates correct from wrong SQL (AUC), and whether the most
  negative `d_t` falls in the wrong clause. Select the teacher.
- **S1 — screen, 2,000 public rows, one pass, from the same adapter.** Arms:
  C-RL, OPD with the S0c teacher, and OPD with T2. Evaluate all five sets
  before any terminal stage and record KL drift from the parent.
  Gate: OPD − C-RL ≥ +1.5 BIRD EX and Spider-family mean not below C-RL.
- **S2 — full run.** All 9,428 rows plus terminal private stage, compared with
  `A>K[ce]>A`. First run a 200-row smoke to measure throughput: the server has
  no vLLM and prompts reach 7,168 tokens, so prefill dominates cost.
- **Stop rule.** If OPD ≤ C-RL, stop teacher-scored transfer and move to
  teacher-generated data (section 7).

## 7. Alternatives

- **Teacher as data generator.** This is where gold CE cannot compete.
  - Label budget: keep gold for only 10% or 25% of public rows; compare
    gold-only with gold plus teacher pseudo-labels filtered without gold
    (executes, non-empty result, self-consistency). Existing teacher
    generations cover about 7.8k executable rows.
  - Synthetic public data: generate question/SQL pairs on public databases,
    SQL first, then question (OmniSQL style), in Spider style without
    evidence.
  - Robustness augmentation aimed at Realistic/SYN (Text2SQL-Flow style).
- **Retention in the terminal stage.** Clients distill from the post-K global
  SLM (FedNTD/FedGKD style): no LLM at clients, no extra communication. Prefer
  this over client-side SDFT, which reports weak teachers at 3B and roughly 4×
  wall-clock cost.
- **Cheaper OPD.** Lightning OPD style: sample per chunk, score once, store
  only top-K. Entropy-based token selection (TIP) reduces memory.
- **Self-anchor in the public stage.** Gold CE plus KL to the pre-K student.
  Doubles as the regularization control for hypothesis 3.

## 8. Deprioritized

- More offline Hinton depth or schedules: lowest transfer tier, with teacher
  hacking risk. SeqKD depth is also deferred until matched selected-gold CE
  establishes teacher-specific value.
- Offline top-K cache for forward KL: biased (Sparse Logit Sampling). If
  forward KL stays, use random-sampling KD.
- Chain-of-thought distillation (Struct-SQL, FINER-SQL): high ceiling (FINER-SQL
  reports 63.17 BIRD dev EX at 1.5B), but it changes the output format,
  increases latency, and widens scope. Those numbers also train on Spider/BIRD
  training data and are not directly comparable.
- OmniSQL-7B as teacher: it trains on Spider and BIRD training sets, which are
  the private data here.

## 9. Risks

- Novelty: see the claim boundary in section 5.
- No teacher-free RL baseline exists in the project yet.
- HF generation throughput on the Windows server is unmeasured.
- All evidence above is single-seed.

## 10. Terminal retention (P2.9): literature and design check

Researched 2026-09-25 before the P2.9 launch.

**Problem.** Plain terminal A trains only on private Spider SQL. It moves the
model toward the Spider optimum from any starting point. Two effects follow:
the arms converge, and the BIRD knowledge transferred at K is partly lost.
The goal of P2.9 is to keep the post-K behaviour while the clients learn
Spider.

**Nearest methods.** Every method in the table distils an earlier model into
the model being trained, using only the current data:

| Method | Client/local objective | Teacher | Reported setting |
|---|---|---|---|
| LwF (Li & Hoiem 2016) | CE(new task) + λ·KL(old ‖ new) on new-task inputs | model before fine-tuning | λ = 1, T = 2 |
| FedGKD (Yao et al., IEEE TPDS 2023) | CE + γ/2·KL(global ‖ local) on local data | last global model (M = 1 recommended for NLP) | γ = 0.2 on DistilBERT, so the weight is 0.1 |
| FedNTD (Lee et al., NeurIPS 2022) | CE + β·τ²·KL(global ‖ local) on non-true classes only | global model | β = 1, τ = 1 (official config) |
| RL's Razor (2025) | analysis only | base model | forgetting follows the KL between the fine-tuned and base model on new-task data |
| WiSE-FT (Wortsman et al., CVPR 2022) | no loss; weight interpolation after fine-tuning | pre-fine-tuning weights | α ≈ 0.5; +4–6 OOD points on ImageNet shifts without ID loss |

**Check of the P2.9 design against these methods.**
- Matches: forward KL(reference ‖ student); reference = frozen post-K global
  model (FedGKD with M = 1); computed on private inputs only (LwF, FedGKD); T =
  1 (FedNTD config). RL's Razor gives the rationale for limiting the KL on
  new-task data.
- Adapted to sequence models: the KL is averaged over SQL target positions
  under teacher forcing, like the Hinton implementation.
- λ: 1.0 matches LwF and FedNTD but is 10× FedGKD's NLP weight. P2.9 therefore
  runs both 1.0 and 0.1.
- Not tested yet: the FedNTD non-true variant, which removes the gold token so
  that retention does not oppose CE. Add it only if λ = 0.1 still blocks the
  Spider repair.

**Competitors reviewers will expect.**
- WiSE-FT, evaluation only, exact for LoRA via rank concatenation. It is in
  P2.9.
- FedProx (weight-space L2). The code exists. Its earlier Spider-only result
  was negative, so it is not rerun here.

**Claim boundary.** The loss is not new. The contribution is the placement: the
retention teacher is the global model **after LLM-to-SLM public KD**, and the
goal is to keep cross-domain transfer through private consolidation, not to
reduce client drift. The paper must cite LwF, FedGKD, FedNTD, and WiSE-FT, and
report the λ trade-off rather than one tuned value.

## 11. Ordering KD and FL (D1 and the ordering study)

Researched 2026-09-25.

**Diagnosis.** `A → K → A` learns two distributions one after the other. In
continual learning, this is the setting most prone to forgetting. The usual
ranking of remedies is:

```text
joint training (upper bound) > replay > regularization (LwF, EWC) > post-hoc merging
```

P2.9 retention sits in the regularization tier. Public BIRD data is not
private, so separating K and A into phases is a design choice, not a privacy
constraint.

**Candidate orders.**

| Order | Idea | Precedent | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A → K → A` + retention | regularize the terminal stage | LwF, FedGKD | covers one round; K knowledge fades over more FL rounds |
| `K → Aᴿ` | KD as the FL initialization | FedMD (public first, then private); [Nguyen et al., ICLR 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.15387): a pre-trained initialization reduces the harm of heterogeneity | K knowledge can fade over R rounds |
| `(A → K)ᴿ` | server KD every round | FedCoLLM, FedDF | expensive (about 5 h per K on 5,319 rows); repeated public damage |
| `A_mixᴿ` | replay public KD targets at clients | experience replay, a strong continual-learning baseline ([TRACE](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.06762), [scalable replay](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.12512)) | extra client compute on long BIRD prompts |
| `Aᴿ + λ·τ_K` | KD as a task vector added to FL | [task arithmetic](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/task-arithmetic-ta); LoRA merging against forgetting ([Merge before Forget](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.23017)) | interference between task vectors |

**D1 (in P2.9, evaluation only).** θ_{A>A} + λ·(θ_{A>K} − θ_A) with
λ ∈ {0.5, 1.0}, for SeqKD and matched gold. Every adapter already exists.
LoRA combination is exact via rank concatenation. The answer selects the
ordering study: an additive task vector favours Merge and K-first; interference
favours Replay and Sequential with retention.

**Claim boundary.** None of these orders is new on its own. A paper
contribution would be the controlled comparison of where LLM-to-SLM KD should
enter a federated Text-to-SQL pipeline, compute-matched against FL with the
same rounds, with teacher targets compared to gold targets in each design.
