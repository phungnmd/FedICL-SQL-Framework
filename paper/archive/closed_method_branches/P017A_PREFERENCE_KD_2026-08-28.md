# P1.7a execution-verified preference KD — closed negative

## Question and fixed gate

P1.7a asked whether positive-only verified teacher-target CE could be improved
by adding a reference-free pairwise loss that ranks the teacher SQL over the
failed SQL generated by the pre-server global FL model. The fixed screen used
the same 512 public rows and positive-only CE control, 347 `global_fl`
rejections, length-normalized sequence scores, and
`lambda_preference = 1.0`.

Promotion required at least `+1.0` Spider EX over the matched positive-only CE
control and no increase in execution errors. The coefficient, pair eligibility,
subset, and update budget were not eligible for post-result tuning.

## Result and decision

| Arm | Spider EX | EM |
|---|---:|---:|
| Positive-only teacher-target CE (`positive_ce512`) | 56.9 | 31.9 |
| CE + global-model preference loss (`global_pref512`) | 54.9 | 26.9 |

The preference arm is approximately `-2.0` EX points at the displayed
precision, so it fails the primary gate regardless of its execution-error
count. No full-3,873 extension or hyperparameter/subset tuning is authorized.
EM is secondary and does not affect this decision.

This result rejects the tested objective, not preference KD as a general
research family. The project-level interpretation is narrower: explicit
negative-sequence ranking did not improve the already-effective verified hard
teacher targets in this matched screen.
